Tin Whistle Note Creator is made for people who play the D tin whistle (high D) and for those just starting out. Pick a song, see its six-hole fingering chart, hear it played with the app's own tin whistle sound and play along with your microphone.

CATALOG
• Approved and community arrangements, search by song or artist
• Fingering charts showing covered, half-covered and open holes for every note
• Tin whistle player: play, pause, BPM, metronome and active-note tracking
• Bundled songs that open without an internet connection

PRACTICE
• Free play: shows the note your microphone hears, its Do/Re/Mi name, octave and fingering
• Song follow: a correct note reveals its fingering and name; a wrong note keeps you on the same step
• Optional guide that plays the next note for you

TUTORIAL
• A 24-step path from your first sound to short tunes
• Note highlighting that moves with the example as you listen
• Finish lessons with your microphone; progress is kept on your device

CONVERT TO NOTES
• Type notes: Do Re Mi or C D E
• Import MIDI and MusicXML files
• Turn a selected part of an audio file into notes
• Search the web for scores and see their source and tempo

COMMUNITY (optional)
• Share your own arrangement, built by typing note names or tapping the holes
• Rate versions with half stars
• Report inappropriate content, block members and delete your account at any time

OUR MASCOT
A goofy but determined donkey plays along: blissful on a right note, baffled on a wrong one, and winking when a lesson is done.

PRIVACY
Microphone audio is processed only on your device; it is never recorded or sent anywhere. No ads, no tracking. Catalog, Practice and Tutorial need no account.

Available in English and Turkish. Written notes are for the high D whistle: written E4 sounds as E5.
